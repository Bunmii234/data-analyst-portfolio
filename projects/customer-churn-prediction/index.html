Customer Churn Prediction

Classifying which customers are at risk of not returning, using a time-based feature/outcome split to avoid leakage — features come from before a cutoff date, the churn label from what actually happened after it.

Honest caveat: 77.3% of customers churned by this definition — a model that just always guesses "churned" already scores 77.3% accuracy. That's why ROC-AUC and precision/recall are reported alongside accuracy: the real test is whether the model beats a random guess (AUC 0.50), not whether it beats the majority-class baseline. Some of that high churn rate also reflects one-time buyers who were never going to reorder, not just customers who disengaged — a real limitation of this label definition, not a hidden one.
